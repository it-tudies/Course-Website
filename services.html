Services Offered

At Kiwi Exploration Tours we offer some of the best service to help you explore New Zealand and the vibrant Māori Culture and Values. Two of our most popular services includ our Guided Cultural Tours and Eco-Tourism Experiences.

People enjoy our Guided Cultural Tours because these provide some great insights into the Māori way of life. We visit some of the most significant Māori sights, and you get to experience what it truly means to be Māori.

Our Eco-Tourism experiences are a favourite among the many of us that share a genuine interest in climate and ideas around what good practices really are. Māori Culture and Values involve thinking about our environment and ourselves as being a collective community if you like.
